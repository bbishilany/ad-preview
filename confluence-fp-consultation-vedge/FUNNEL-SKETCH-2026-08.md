# CFP Meta — Sequenced Funnel (NET-NEW TEST, runs alongside v2)

**Author:** Rania Khalil, Dir. Paid Media · **Date:** 2026-08-13
**Status:** Strategy sketch. Nothing built, no budget committed. The v2 always-on set is UNTOUCHED — this is a separate campaign we test next to it (client buys in on v2 first).
**Objective (unchanged from v2):** book HNW/UHNW consultations on CFP's live consultation page. Primary metric = cost-per-qualified-consultation (CPQC), so the two approaches read head-to-head.

## Thesis
v2 asks "can one sharp ad book a consultation?" This funnel asks "does staging the same concepts as a conversation — recognition, then the coordinated-plan reveal as the payoff, then the ask — book a better-fit consultation for less?" It's the v2 insight (coordination is the payoff, not the hook) turned into stages.

## Honest headline
A sequenced funnel lives or dies on WARM-POOL VOLUME, and CFP is a niche HNW audience in one metro. That is the whole risk. Design front-loads top-of-funnel and gives hard thresholds for when stages 2/3 turn on. If warm pools starve, flat v2 wins — and that itself is a cheap, useful finding.

## 1. Stage architecture (3 stages; 2 & 3 conditional on pool size)

**Stage 1 — Cold hook (build the qualified pool)**
- Job: situational recognition hook to net-new Pittsburgh HNW; capture leaners-in as a retargetable pool. The POOL is the product, not clicks.
- Objective: Engagement → Video Views (ThruPlay); video-viewer CAs accumulate faster/cheaper than LP pools in a thin niche. Static secondary for link clicks.
- Audience (cold): Pittsburgh MSA, ~45–70, broad/lightly-shaped — creative qualifies (a business-sale ad is invisible to a salaried retiree). Lookalike off a CFP client list only if client shares + compliance clears; else skip.
- v2 concepts here (door-openers): Business Sale, Concentrated Stock, Equity Comp, Second Opinion. Throttled relief-valve on-ramps: Retirement Timing, Plan for the Unplanned (volume, but wronger leads — not the lead).

**Stage 2 — Warm / proof (deliver the payoff)**
- Job: to leaners-in, deliver the turn — why a consultation with CFP beats a one-off answer. Coordination-as-payoff LIVES here.
- Objective: Traffic (link clicks) to the consultation page (or Engagement to deepen pool first).
- Audience (entry CAs): video viewers ≥50% (365d) and ≥75% (hotter); FB Page + IG engagers (365d); Stage 1 link clickers.
- v2 concepts: "The Whole Picture / Managed in Pieces, or as One?" (the one differentiator ad — belongs WARM, not cold); Second Opinion re-served warm.

**Stage 3 — Consultation ask (close)**
- Job: direct low-friction invite to those who've seen concern + payoff. No new persuasion.
- Objective: Leads/Conversions on the booking event.
- Audience (entry): consultation-page visitors (180d), all site visitors (180d), 95% video viewers, form-starters (if page fires it).
- v2 concepts: Legacy ("Leave a Plan They Can Follow") as closing beat + a clean consultation-ask creative, button = Request a Consultation.

## 2. Exclusions / flow control
- Stage 1 excludes everyone in Stage 2 & 3 pools (cold chases net-new only).
- Stage 2 excludes Stage 3 pools + converters.
- Stage 3 excludes converters.
- Converters exit ALL: one "Booked Consultation" exclusion (thank-you URL / Schedule event, 180d) as a negative on all three sets. Single most important control.
- Cross-campaign overlap with v2 is expected (same pool). Rec: accept it, read blended CPQC + last-touch, don't handicap either campaign with exclusions.

## 3. Creative implications
| Stage | Format | Reuse | NET-NEW |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Video-led (6–15s) + static | v2 situational hooks | Cut those hooks as short, silent-legible, captioned video (main new lift) |
| 2 | Short video / clean static | v2 "Whole Picture" + "Second Opinion" | A proof/credibility asset (independent, fiduciary, CFP® professional, whole-picture) — NOT results/returns |
| 3 | Static, direct | v2 Legacy + CTA | Minimal consultation-ask creative, message-matched to live page |

Compliance guardrail on Stage 2 proof asset: proof = credibility & process, NEVER results/returns/performance. Same gates as v2 (Serena + Katie/Marissa). CFP® body only; no CFA; no "coordinate" as hook.

## 4. Budget + sequencing logic (volume truth)
- Pittsburgh MSA ~2.3M: cold reach isn't the bottleneck (creative filters). WARM-POOL accumulation is.
- CAs need ~1,000+ to serve efficiently. Video-viewer pool → ~2–4 wks; LP-visitor pool → 4–8 wks, and in this niche MAY never reach standalone size.
- Launch sequenced, not all at once:
  1. Wks 0–3: Stage 1 only (~100% budget). Build pool.
  2. Turn on Stage 2 when ≥50% video pool clears ~1,000–2,000 → ~60/30/10.
  3. Turn on Stage 3 when LP+95% pool clears ~500–1,000 → ~50/30/20 steady state.
  4. If Stage 3 never clears: fold it into Stage 2 as a high-bid segment. 3-set → 2-set in a thin niche is the correct outcome, not failure.
- Mechanics: ABO with a floor per warm set (opposite of v2's one-broad-set — that difference is what we test).
- Client honesty: own budget line on TOP of v2; first bookings arrive LATER than v2 (warm stages fill first). Below a floor, run 2-set or don't run it.

## 5. Measurement
- Primary (both campaigns): cost-per-qualified-consultation; qualified judged on intake (HNW-by-situation), same definition as v2 → apples-to-apples.
- Leading indicators: S1 cost/50%+75% view, warm-pool growth rate, 3-sec hook rate; S2 warm CTR, LP-visit rate, cost/LP visitor; S3 consultation-start rate, cost/booking, booking→qualified rate.
- Head-to-head: funnel vs flat-v2 as two separate concurrent campaigns, even split, same destination + event, 6–8 wk read. Compare blended CPQC + qualified-booking rate. Caveat: one metro = no clean geo split, low counts = read directionally + qualitatively. Don't kill Stage 3 for "no conversions" before its pool fills.

## 6. Risks / honest cautions
1. Pool starvation (#1 failure mode) — niche + single metro; Stage 3 may never justify its own set.
2. Thesis depends on volume it may not get — if warm starves, payoff never delivered.
3. Frequency/fatigue spikes fast in small HNW pools.
4. Longer time-to-signal than v2.
5. Attribution muddiness at low volume.
6. Meta's algo prefers consolidation (v2 is already that) — we're betting against the default; pays only if staged messaging lifts QUALITY.
7. Bigger compliance surface (proof asset most likely to drift toward performance language).

## 7. How to pitch to the client
- Alongside v2, never instead. v2 stays as is. Separate campaign, own budget line.
- One-liner: "v2 is the sharpest single shot. The funnel is the same concepts staged as a conversation — recognition first, the coordinated-plan reveal as the payoff, the ask last. Run side by side, let cost-per-qualified-consultation pick the winner."
- Two honest expectations: own budget; first bookings arrive later.
- What we're buying: whether sequencing lowers CPQC or raises booking quality enough to justify complexity. If not, learned cheaply; keep v2 as workhorse.
- Compliance identical to v2.
