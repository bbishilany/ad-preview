## The question on the table
Client's marketing lead pushed back on the current 10-ad set: simplify, add edge, **one ad = one concept / one concern**, drop the "coordinating everything under one roof" theme (too conceptual, industry-driven), fix unnatural phrasing (you don't "build" an estate/taxes). Bill's tension: *"she's almost suggesting no arc just concepts, and I'm not sure how I feel about that."*

## The grounded answer (all four agreed, independently)
**The arc does not die. It relocates and compresses.** Today the emotional arc runs *across the campaign* (four territories building a "coordination" story) with the **firm** as protagonist. The move is to put a compressed **two-beat micro-arc inside each ad** — **Recognition → Question → (invite)** — with the **reader** as protagonist. The client's own examples are arcs ("worked 30 years… how do you know when you have enough?" = setup → destabilizing question). So Bill's instinct (never go arc-less) and the client's instinct (one felt concern, hook-first) are **the same instinct**. What she's actually killing is the *catalog* (four financial domains stacked in the opening line), not the arc.

**The differentiator moves from the hook to the payoff.** "Coordination" is CFP's real differentiator, but it's a *mechanism* — it describes what the firm does, not what the prospect feels at 7am. So: retire "coordinate/coordinating" from all ad surfaces (hooks, headlines, story overlays); keep the capability as the **turn** inside body copy + on the destination page — the reason a consultation with CFP beats a one-off answer they could get anywhere. *The hook is the door; the differentiator is the room.*

## The one guardrail we hold against the client
Her sample concepts (retirement "enough," "unplanned") are **mass-affluent**, not HNW. Sharper hooks cut both ways: a hook any 62-year-old relates to books cheaper, *wronger* leads. Fix = **concept selection is the positioning** — lead-weight concerns that are HNW-by-situation (business sale, concentrated stock, equity comp) or whose honest answer requires the whole picture. Edge here = **precision, not volume** (compliance bans urgency/scarcity/claims anyway).

## Recommended portfolio (~6 core + 2 rounding — one felt concern each)
Budget-weighted to the situation concepts. All compliant (no urgency/scarcity/guarantees/performance/dollar minimums); CFP® in body only; HNW by situation.

|-----|---------|----------------------|-----------------|

## Also flagged
- **Natural phrasing:** you don't *build* an estate or taxes — you *leave* an estate / *build* an estate plan / *save* for retirement / *manage* taxes. Rule: one verb, one object, the verb people actually use. No 4-item noun-lists in the opener (that's the concept-stacking tell). Live errors in current Ad 1 + carousel.
- **CTA inconsistency (fix on rebuild):** feed button is "Learn More" on all 10 while the Stories sticker correctly says "Request a Consultation." On a consultation-objective campaign to a consultation page, switch the button to **Request a Consultation** on the high-intent concepts; keep "Learn More" only as a test on the softest on-ramp.
- **Ad-set architecture (Rania):** consolidate — one campaign, one broad ad set; let the *creative* segment. Test at **concept level** first, then hook, then image. Judge on **cost-per-qualified-consultation + lead quality**, not CTR (the broad "unplanned" hook will win CTR and lose on qualified bookings).
- **Message-match (Marcus):** the sharper the ad, the higher the bar for the generic destination page. Hold concepts at "concern" level so the current page still matches; only fund concept-anchored LP sections if we scale the narrow situational hooks hard.
