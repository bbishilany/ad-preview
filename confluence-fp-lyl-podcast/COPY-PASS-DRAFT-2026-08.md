## The 12 (line breaks = \n\n on apply)

**Ad 1 · Jason Esch · "Leadership Gaps in the Next Generation"**
Jason Esch runs an IT company, but the first thing he wants the next generation of leaders to learn has nothing to do with technology.
Empathy over ego. Inspiration over control. Diversity of thought.
Three lessons he keeps coming back to.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 2 · Jason Esch · "What My Son Taught Me"**
Jason Esch leads a company. Some of his clearest lessons on leadership came from his son.
Legacy is not what you leave behind, he says. It is how you live it, every day.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 3 · Bill Bishilany · "Build a Life that Starts with Happiness"**
Most people chase success and expect happiness to follow.
Bill Bishilany believes it works the other way around.
Everything you have ever wanted is on the other side of happiness, he says. Be happy first.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 4 · Rachel Petrucelli · "What Truly Fills Her Cup"**
After 22 years raising money for a children's hospital, Rachel Petrucelli knows exactly what fills her cup.
It is the privilege of helping someone turn their own hard-earned money into a difference in a child's life.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 5 · Bill Bishilany · "The Mosaic"**
There is no single thing that makes a business grow online, Bill Bishilany says.
He describes it as a mosaic. Small tiles that only mean something once they come together.
New episodes every two weeks.

**Ad 6 · Bill Bishilany · "The Power of Collective Experience"**
Construction. Medical devices. Franchise turnarounds. Digital marketing.
Bill Bishilany's path looks scattered until you hear why he named his company Mileage.
Every experience adds mileage, he says. And more mileage makes you better at the next problem.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 7 · Bill Sarris · "It Takes a Team (Sarris Candies)"**
Bill Sarris grew Sarris Candies from a family basement to nearly 500 people.
You cannot build something lasting alone, he says.
Some of his associates have stayed 45 and 50 years. That is not an accident.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 8 · Bill Sarris · "Sarris Candies Story"**  _(Serena: strongest in set)_
It started in a basement in Canonsburg. Chocolate bunnies made six at a time.
Today Sarris Candies turns out hundreds of thousands of them every Easter.
We were never really selling chocolate, Bill Sarris says. The chocolate was the tool.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 9 · Ezra Shapiro · "A Career of Continuous Learning"**
Ezra Shapiro left a finance career to peel potatoes in a restaurant kitchen.
Years later, he was cooking in a three Michelin star kitchen.
The people who think they have it all figured out, he says, are the boring ones.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 10 · Ezra Shapiro · "Learning Through Reading"**
Before Ezra Shapiro ever worked in a kitchen, he read.
Chef biographies. Cookbooks. Books on how a restaurant actually runs.
Read everything you can about the thing you want to do, he says. It is the most important part.
New episodes every two weeks.

**Ad 11 · Ezra Shapiro · "Not Just Dinner, a Curated Experience"**  _(Serena: quietest hook; optional polish)_
For Ezra Shapiro, a private dinner was never just about the food.
It is the room, the people, the occasion, and a menu built for that night alone.
The part he loves most is the relationships around the table.
Watch on YouTube.

**Ad 12 · Rachel Petrucelli · "The Margin of Excellence"**
Donations do not keep the lights on at a children's hospital, Rachel Petrucelli says.
They fund what she calls the margin of excellence.
The specialists who keep care close to home. The things that make a child's stay less scary.
Watch on YouTube.
